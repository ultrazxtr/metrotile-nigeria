About MetroTile Nigeria Ltd

We are a trusted roofing company based in Abuja specializing in high-quality stone coated metal roofing tiles.

We supply and install durable, beautiful, and weather-resistant roofing systems for residential and commercial buildings across Nigeria.

Our goal is to give every client a roof they can be proud of for decades.
